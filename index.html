THE CINNAMON CHRONICLES • SRI LANKA ORIGIN

95% of People Buy the Wrong Cinnamon.
Here Is Why True Bark Is In A League Of Its Own.

Over 95% of supermarket cinnamon is cheap, bitter Cassia bark. Real Ceylon cinnamon is rare, handcrafted, and naturally sweet. Explore the visual chapters below to see the difference.

CHAPTER 01 • THE REVELATION

You've Been Buying The Wrong Cinnamon.

Most people go their entire lives without ever tasting genuine cinnamon. In supermarket spice aisles across the US and Europe, nearly every jar labeled "cinnamon" is actually Cassia—a pungent, coarse tree bark from China and Indonesia.

Botanical Fact

True cinnamon comes from only one plant on Earth: Cinnamomum verum, indigenous exclusively to the southern coast of Sri Lanka.

CHAPTER 02 • THE SUPERMARKET FLOOD

95% Of People Buy The Wrong Cinnamon.

Standing in a forest of dark, hollow, mass-produced Cassia quills, real Ceylon cinnamon glows alone. Less than 5% of global cinnamon trade is authentic Sri Lankan Ceylon.

Why Mass Brands Sell Cassia

Cassia trees are clear-cut with machinery, yielding thick woody bark at a fraction of the cost. True Ceylon requires artisanal hand-harvesting by generational craft peelers.

CHAPTER 03 • THE ANATOMY TEST

It Looks Similar, But Isn't.

Inspect the physical structure. Cassia is a single, thick, hollow log that is rock-hard and bitter. Ceylon is composed of dozens of paper-thin, cigar-rolled inner bark layers.

CASSIA
  • Thick rough bark
  • One hard layer
  • Strong & sharp burn
  • High coumarin (up to 1%)
CEYLON (TRUE BARK)
  • Thin whisper bark
  • Many delicate layers
  • Sweet & floral aroma
  • Negligible coumarin (<0.004%)
CHAPTER 04 • MICROSCOPIC DETAIL

Look Inside.

When you cut open an authentic Ceylon quill, you see a majestic concentric spiral of paper-thin layers rolled by hand. It resembles a tightly rolled Havana cigar rather than tree wood.

Why It Dissolves in Coffee

Because these inner layers are soft and pliable (not dense outer wood), stone-milling creates a velvety micro-powder that dissolves into espresso crema without gritty sediment.

CHAPTER 05 • GENERATIONAL HERITAGE

The Layers Never Lie.

In the southern estates of Galle and Matara, cinnamon peeling is an honored artisan lineage. Peelers shave the golden inner bark in continuous translucent strips, rolling over 50 delicate layers into a single quill before sun-drying.

Counterfeit-Proof Nature

Industrial machines cannot duplicate these micro-layers. Every True Bark quill is an unbroken testament to authentic Sri Lankan heritage.

CHAPTER 06 • THE ALBA STANDARD

The Power Of Simple.

Alba is the rarest and most expensive grade of Ceylon cinnamon on the planet. Selected from pencil-thin, youthful inner bark quills, Alba delivers an unparalleled floral bouquet and sweet citrus finish.
