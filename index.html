THE TRUE BARK FIELD JOURNAL • MATARA & GALLE HIGHLANDS (6.05°N, 80.22°E)

From Sacred Inner Cambium to Velvet Espresso Crema.
Six Chapters of Sri Lankan Craft, Botanical Science & Barista Ritual.

Why do specialty coffee roasters and Michelin-trained pastry chefs refuse ordinary supermarket bark? Trace the six-stage artisanal journey of True Bark Cinnamomum verum from monsoon-fed sapling to your 100g keepsake cylinder tin.

MONOGRAPH I • THE MONSOON TERROIR

Born on the Mist-Covered Slopes of Southern Ceylon.

True botanical cinnamon (Cinnamomum verum) thrives in only one microclimate on Earth: the mineral-rich, sandy-loam coastal hills of Matara and Galle in southern Sri Lanka. Here, twin Indian Ocean monsoons and equatorial sunshine infuse two-year-old saplings with delicate citrus, wild honey, and orchid-like essential oils.

Single-Origin Estate Traceability

Unlike mass-market spices blended from industrial timber yards across Asia, every True Bark tin and window pouch is harvested directly from family-tended gardens along Sri Lanka's southern spice belt.

MONOGRAPH II • BOTANICAL ANATOMY

0.3mm Living Phloem vs. Coarse Outer Bark.

Ordinary grocery store spice is ground from the thick, dead outer trunk cortex of Cinnamomum cassia trees. True Bark discards the rough outer bark completely—harvesting only the 0.3mm living inner phloem (cambium) where sweet volatile esters and aromatic oils are concentrated.

Why the Inner Cambium Matters

Because the inner cambium contains zero woody cork tissue, it yields a naturally sweet, golden-amber ribbon with a warm floral bouquet and none of the harsh, tongue-numbing bite of outer tree bark.

MONOGRAPH III • THE 4:00 AM PEELING GUILD

Hand-Curled at Dawn Into Telescopic Alba Quills.

Harvest begins at 4:00 AM when natural sap moisture peaks. Master peelers massage each cut shoot with a polished brass rod to loosen the cambium, shave whisper-thin ribbons with a curved kokatta knife, and telescope 50+ fragile curls inside one another by hand.

INDUSTRIAL CASSIA LOGS
  • Machine-stripped outer trunk
  • Single hollow woody tube
  • Harsh, peppery throat burn
  • High coumarin (up to 1.0%)
TRUE BARK CEYLON QUILLS
  • Hand-shaved 0.3mm cambium
  • 50+ telescopic cigar layers
  • Sweet honey-citrus warmth
  • Negligible coumarin (<0.004%)
MONOGRAPH IV • COLD-STONE MICRO-MILLING

Slow-Ground Below 38°C to Protect Volatile Oils.

High-speed commercial hammer mills generate friction temperatures above 90°C—scorching delicate spice powders and evaporating up to 60% of their essential oils before packaging. True Bark uses slow, cold-stone micro-milling kept strictly below 38°C.

4.2% Essential Oil Retention

By milling in small, temperature-controlled batches and sealing immediately into our gold-rimmed 100g composite cans and barrier pouches, every spoonful retains its freshly harvested Sri Lankan aroma.

MONOGRAPH V • BARISTA CREMA SCIENCE

Velvet Solubility with Zero Gritty Sludge.

Have you ever noticed how supermarket cinnamon turns into a gummy, sandy sludge at the bottom of your coffee cup? That is insoluble outer wood cellulose. Because True Bark is milled exclusively from tender inner phloem to a 60-mesh superfine silk, it suspends seamlessly in hot espresso crema and steamed milk.

Crafted for Specialty Coffee

Whether whisked into a double-shot cortado, dusted over oat milk microfoam, or folded into pastry custard, True Bark elevates natural coffee sweetness without masking single-origin bean notes.

MONOGRAPH VI • BIOCHEMICAL PURITY

250× Lower Coumarin for Daily Morning Rituals.

Lab-Verified <0.004% Coumarin • 100% Pure Cinnamomum verum • Zero Adulterants

Beyond flavor lies vital biochemistry. Common Cassia bark contains up to 1.00% coumarin—a naturally occurring hepatotoxin that health authorities warn against consuming daily. True Bark Ceylon Cinnamon is lab-certified at <0.004% coumarin (over 250 times lower).

The True Bark Purity Guarantee

Enjoy generous daily servings in your morning espresso, protein smoothies, and overnight oats with complete peace of mind—backed by single-estate Sri Lankan certification.
