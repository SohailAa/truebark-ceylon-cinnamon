ROOTS IN SOUTHERN SRI LANKA

Preserving Centuries of Bark Peeling Artistry

In the lush hillside valleys of Galle and Matara, cinnamon harvesting is not an industrial process—it is a hereditary craft passed down across five generations.

Unlike mass-market spices that sit in dusty warehouses for years, True Bark is harvested in small seasonal flushes. The outer coarse rind is peeled away by hand with a brass blade, revealing the tender, fragrant inner parchment. These whisper-thin layers are rolled into quills and sun-dried under tropical trade winds before immediate hermetic sealing.
